# Product: Creature Voice Studio — Monster Voice Laboratory

Publisher: **F7 Developer**. Android app id: `com.f7developer.creaturevoicestudio`.
Built for **Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest category**: the app is a "costume" for your voice.

## Promise
A premium, fully offline monster-voice laboratory. Every voice is synthesised by a physical
model of an airway (lungs, vocal folds, throat, mouth, nose) running in Rust/WebAssembly, then
spliced with creature "DNA" by our own Rust DSP. No samples, no cloud, no paid APIs.

## Feature pillars (all must be real, never placeholders)
1. **Creature DNA Mixer** — blend Monster, Alien, Robot, Human and Animal strands; the blend
   drives the physical voice source and a Rust effect rack, and splices a real animal airway
   (songbird, seal, alligator, elephant) into the voice.
2. **Interactive 3D Specimen** — eight sculpted creature heads on a glowing pedestal; jaw, lips
   and tongue move from engine telemetry in real time, and the DNA grows horns, antennae, a visor
   or a collar. (The earlier airway-anatomy hologram was removed: no anatomy diagrams.)
3. **Text-to-Voice** — English (CMUdict), Indonesian, Spanish, raw IPA, and "Creature Tongue".
4. **Singing Studio**, **Beatbox Maker**, **Sound FX Generator**.
5. **Voice Morphing** — morph between two characters, and morph your own microphone voice.
6. Character presets, a clip editor, project storage, WAV and MP3 export, share sheet.

## Audience and tone
This is the **global edition**: worldwide cosplayers, streamers, game modders, Halloween party
hosts, kids with parents. Everything — UI copy, docs, store listing, demo, code comments — is
**English only**, short, playful-sinister lab language ("Splice", "Incubate", "Specimen").
Visual theme follows Kiroween globally: Halloween night, a haunted futuristic lab, ghost-purple
and toxic-green neon. Speech languages (English, Indonesian, Spanish, IPA, Creature Tongue) are
content options, not UI locales.

## Non-goals
No accounts, no network calls, no ads SDK, no analytics, no copyrighted audio in the repo.

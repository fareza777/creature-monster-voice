# Architecture

The full design is in [`.kiro/specs/creature-voice-studio/design.md`](../.kiro/specs/creature-voice-studio/design.md).
The vendored engine is described in [`docs/upstream/architecture.md`](upstream/architecture.md)
and [`docs/upstream/rust-engine.md`](upstream/rust-engine.md). This page is the short version.

## Three threads, one wasm module

`creature.wasm` (Rust, built for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, no imports) is compiled once and
instantiated three times:

| Thread | Instance used for |
|---|---|
| UI (React) | Small JSON calls: planning, G2P previews, designs. |
| Render worker | Offline renders: the CHIMERA engine plus the DNA effect rack and the animal airway splice. Streams PCM and 60 fps telemetry frames; encodes WAV and MP3. |
| AudioWorklet (48 kHz) | Live voice (Incubate pad, beatbox pads) and the microphone morph monitor. |

The physical airway is too heavy to run in real time on a Snapdragon 680 WebView with an animal
airway spliced in, so speech, songs and effects are rendered in the worker and played back.
Playback starts before the render ends once enough audio is buffered.

## Crates

| Crate | Origin | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `crates/chimera` | CHIMERA (MIT), vendored unchanged | Physical voice engine: airway, glottis, articulators, planners, English G2P, creature airways. |
| `crates/chimera-wasm` | CHIMERA (MIT), vendored unchanged | The engine's C ABI. |
| `crates/creature-lab` | Original | DNA model and effect rack, Indonesian/Spanish G2P, Creature Tongue, SFX synth, editor ops, WAV, offline renderer with telemetry, JSON API. |
| `crates/creature-wasm` | Original | One cdylib: the upstream ABI plus the `lab_*` ABI. |

## 3D specimen

`src/three/specimen.ts` renders one of eight heads (`public/models/<kind>.glb`, generated by the
Blender scripts in `tools/blender/`). The jaw, lips and tongue follow the engine's articulator
telemetry; skin, horns, antennae, visor and collar follow the DNA.

## App

React 19 + zustand + three.js in Vite, packaged for Android with Capacitor 8. Projects and clips
are stored in IndexedDB; exports go to `Documents/` and the Android share sheet. The app has no
INTERNET permission.
